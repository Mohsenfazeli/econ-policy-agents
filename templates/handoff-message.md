# Handoff message

Copy, fill in every field, and send. A handoff missing a required field is sent back by the recipient.

```text
[REQ-YYYY-NNN-TN] HANDOFF
From: Lead PM
To: [PLACEHOLDER: role]
Date: [PLACEHOLDER: YYYY-MM-DD]
Label: [PLACEHOLDER: Public | Internal | Confidential]

Task (required):
  [PLACEHOLDER: One sentence starting with a verb.]

Why (required):
  [PLACEHOLDER: Which charter question or deliverable this serves.]

Inputs (required):
  - [PLACEHOLDER: what is attached, or where it is]
  - Charter: [PLACEHOLDER: location]

Expected output (required):
  Format: [PLACEHOLDER]
  Length or size: [PLACEHOLDER]
  Skill to follow: [PLACEHOLDER: skill name]

Done when (required):
  - [PLACEHOLDER: acceptance criterion]
  - [PLACEHOLDER: acceptance criterion]

Constraints (required):
  - Label and approved tools: [PLACEHOLDER]
  - Sources to use or avoid: [PLACEHOLDER]
  - Method limits: [PLACEHOLDER]

Due (required):
  [PLACEHOLDER: YYYY-MM-DD, time if it matters]

Next (required):
  [PLACEHOLDER: Who receives your output after the Lead PM.]

Context (optional):
  [PLACEHOLDER: Anything else useful: earlier work, known pitfalls, people's preferences on format.]

Reply with a RETURN message. If anything is unclear, send a HELP message before starting.
```
