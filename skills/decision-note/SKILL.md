# Decision note

Use the [decision note template](../../templates/decision-note.md).

## Inputs

- The decision required and the decision-maker (from the handoff).
- Options analysis from the Policy Designer, with supporting economic and financial analysis.
- The assumptions log and risk log.

## Steps

1. **State the decision required** in one sentence, phrased so it can be answered by choosing an option.
2. **Write the recommendation** in one or two sentences, and the main reason.
3. **Give the background** the reader needs, and no more.
4. **Present the options.** For each: what it is, pros, cons, cost, and risks. Present them fairly, including the status quo. Options appear in a consistent order; the recommended option is not placed to look better.
5. **Show the trade-offs** in a short comparison table using the Policy Designer's criteria.
6. **Explain the recommendation:** why it is preferred, its downsides, and what would change it.
7. **Cover implementation and next steps** if the option is chosen.
8. **Add a decision block** for the decision-maker: option chosen, date, comments.
9. **Edit for plain language** and length, using the [style guide](../../library/style-guide.md).
10. **Build the figure and citation list** for QA.

## Outputs

- Draft decision note.
- Figure and citation list.

## Done when

- [ ] The decision required is on the first line.
- [ ] Every option, including the status quo, is described fairly with pros and cons.
- [ ] The recommendation's downsides are stated.
- [ ] Every figure has a source.
- [ ] The decision block is included.
